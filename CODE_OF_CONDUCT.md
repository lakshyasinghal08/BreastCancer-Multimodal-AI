# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment
The Multimodal Breast Cancer AI Collaborative Research Consortium is dedicated to providing an inclusive, respectful, and harassment-free environment for all participants, regardless of gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, race, ethnicity, age, religion, or technical background.

## Expected Standards
All contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to:
- Exercise empathy and kindness toward peers.
- Provide and receive constructive, professional scientific critique.
- Respect diverse viewpoints, clinical experiences, and scientific opinions.
- Prioritize ethical AI principles, patient privacy, and data security.
- Avoid derogatory, abusive, or discriminatory behavior in issues, discussions, and code reviews.

## Enforcement & Reporting
Project maintainers are responsible for upholding these community standards. If you observe unacceptable behavior, please contact the ethics and governance board at `clinical-ai-governance@consortium-research.org`. Reports will be investigated promptly and handled with confidentiality.

## Scope
This code of conduct applies to all spaces managed by this research initiative, including repository discussions, code contributions, documentation, and academic collaborations.
